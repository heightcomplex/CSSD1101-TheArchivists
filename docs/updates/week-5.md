### Challenges & risks (severity, mitigation)

- **Interface Usability — Medium:** Large cover panels and inefficient layouts can make browsing multiple series difficult, particularly on mobile devices. 
  - **Mitigation:** Refine the layout, improve spacing and navigation elements, and review the interface across different screen sizes.

- **Genre Categorization and Filtering — Medium:** Broad genre categories and inaccurate tags may prevent users from finding relevant series. 
  - **Mitigation:** Improve the organization of filtering categories, clarify search options, and refine the filtering interface to make content discovery easier.

- **Reading Progress and Chapter Navigation — Medium:** Users may struggle to return to previously read chapters or switch between multiple series efficiently. 
  - **Mitigation:** Refine the personal bookshelf and recent reads interface, improve chapter navigation, and evaluate the experience against the established user stories.
